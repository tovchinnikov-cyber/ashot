A-Shot

A native screenshot tool for Mac on Apple Silicon. Fast, free, no Electron, no tracking.

Why A-Shot

⚡️

Instant launch

Swift + AppKit + ScreenCaptureKit — no Electron, no lag when capturing.

⌨️

Global hotkey

Works even when another app is focused — including Zoom and Teams calls.

🖊️

Annotations

Arrows, rectangles, and text right on the screenshot before copying.

🖥️

Multi-monitor

Select a region on any of your connected displays.

📋

Straight to clipboard

One click or ⌘C — and the screenshot is ready to paste into chat.

🔒

Privacy

No telemetry, no account, no ads. Open source.

Installation

  1. Download A-Shot.dmg and drag A-Shot.app into Applications
  2. On first launch macOS will show a Gatekeeper warning — this is expected, the app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Security and click Open Anyway next to A-Shot
  3. Grant Accessibility and Screen Recording access when macOS asks — without them the hotkey and screen capture won't work
  4. Done — press ⌘⇧S (or your own hotkey from settings) and select a region
